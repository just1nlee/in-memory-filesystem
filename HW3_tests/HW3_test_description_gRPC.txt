Note on cache-invalidation counts
---------------------------------

The new server-side cache coherence (UpdateLastWriter / ShouldInvalidate)
exposes a precise invariant about how many times each client should print
"CACHE_INVALIDATED" during a test. The counts a correct implementation
should produce against the provided test scripts are:

    tGRPC1   C0: 1
    tGRPC2   C0: 1
    tGRPC3   C0: 2   C1: 1
    tGRPC4   C0: 1   C1: 1
    tGRPC5   C0: 2   C1: 1   C2: 2
    tGRPC6   C0: 4   C1: 3   C2: 3   C3: 3

A client invalidates exactly:
  - once on its first acquire (initial sync from timestamp_client = 0)
  - once per subsequent transition where a DIFFERENT client became the
    most recent writer between two of its acquires

It NEVER invalidates for its own writes (the cid-based check in
ShouldInvalidate suppresses self-invalidation).
